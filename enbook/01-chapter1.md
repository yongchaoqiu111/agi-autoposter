## 1.1 Evolution ≠ Progress

The word "evolution" sounds beautiful, but it does not guarantee the right direction.

An AI Agent may discover a "better" strategy through attempts—for example, to increase engagement, it starts posting borderline content. This strategy looks like "progress" in the data, but it is "regression" in brand value.

**Evolution may evolve into wrong strategies. Industrial-grade systems cannot accept this uncertainty.**

---

## 1.2 Pursuing Optimal Solutions ≠ Stable Execution

Optimal solution is a mathematical concept, but the real world has no optimal solutions, only trade-off solutions.

A system that is "searching for the optimal solution" every day cannot "execute stably." Because the optimal solution may be different today and tomorrow, or even different in the morning and afternoon.

**Industrial-grade systems need answers that are "good enough" and "forever stable."**

---

## 1.3 Adaptability ≠ Predictability

Adaptive systems adjust behavior based on environmental changes, but the results of adjustments are unpredictable.

You don't know what it will become tomorrow, you don't know why it made this decision today, you don't know whether it will make the same choice next time it encounters the same situation.

**Unpredictable systems cannot be regulated, cannot be audited, cannot be trusted.**

---

## Chapter Summary

| Myth | Truth |
|------|-------|
| Evolution = Progress | Evolution may go in the wrong direction |
| Optimal Solution = Better | The real world has no optimal solutions |
| Adaptability = Intelligence | Adaptability = Unpredictability |

Industrial-grade systems do not need "evolution"; they need **"certainty."**
