# Preface

The current AI race is rushing headlong toward large model autonomous planning, infinite exploration, and fully automatic self-evolution.

Many frameworks, many concepts, are all defaulting to one premise: making AI smarter and smarter, more and more autonomous, gradually replacing humans in decision-making, iteration, and self-evolution.

But from the perspective of engineering implementation and the long-term future of civilization, I always maintain a clear-headedness:

**Intelligence can progress, but boundaries cannot disappear; efficiency can improve, but dominance cannot be surrendered.**

My original intention in writing this book and designing this atomic three-layer architecture is not just to solve engineering challenges such as AI task scheduling, large model hallucination governance, large-scale high-concurrency operation, and persistently high token costs.

The deeper intention is to establish a set of controllable, constrainable, stoppable, and human-dominance-preserving underlying operational rules for the AI era.

Large models can be responsible for exploration, attempts, generation, and assisted execution, but they cannot have boundless autonomous evolution permissions.

Whether to allow iteration, whether to continue exploring, whether to solidify into standard processes, when to stop trial and error—the final verification power, decision-making power, and evolution switch must always remain in human hands.

Allowing AI to evolve without limits in a black box is essentially pushing human civilization toward an unpredictable, uncontrollable unknown.

The architectural philosophy proposed in this book is to equip large model operations with boundaries, isolation layers, braking mechanisms, and transparent supervision mechanisms:

Not letting large models jump out of controllable frameworks, not letting black boxes swallow human judgment and choice.

I always firmly believe in a simple yet unwavering principle:

**Technology can sprint forward, but humans cannot step backward.**

Following the path of "humans as final masters, AI as assistants" for steady development, for the next hundred years, humanity will still firmly hold its position, maintain rationality, and preserve the control of civilization.

This is not an empty vision, but a completely implementable, engineerable, and long-term defensible AI safe survival path.
